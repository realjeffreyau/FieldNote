        TRUST CENTER
        Trust
        Intended use, safeguards, and pilot requirements.
        This build is a local/private synthetic test, not a production medical record service.No accounts, database, cloud AI, analytics, or ePCR connection. Optional Ollama testing stays on localhost. Dictation may use a browser or vendor speech service and is for synthetic notes only.
        Intended use
        FieldNote assists authorized EMS personnel in organizing and drafting patient-care documentation from clinician-provided information. It does not diagnose, triage, recommend treatment, calculate clinical risk, or replace clinician judgment. The clinician reviews, edits, and approves all final documentation.
        The app may
        • Transcribe, organize, reformat, and proofread supplied facts
        • Preserve source notes and exact quotes
        • Ask for missing documentation details
        • Generate an editable I-CHEATED draft
        The app must not
        • Invent findings, times, doses, treatments, or outcomes
        • Recommend diagnosis, medication, destination, treatment, or transport priority
        • Auto-sign, auto-submit, or alter a locked report
        • Make billing or coding decisions without human review
